Section 13

Video Script

A word-for-word script for the capstone demo video. Target: 3–5 minutes. Tone: confident, clear, professional.

Run Sheet

0:00–0:25Hook & Problem
0:25–0:50Introduce the App
0:50–1:20Sign In & Dashboard
1:20–2:00New Application Wizard
2:00–3:20AI Kit Walkthrough (4 tabs)
3:20–3:50Deployment
3:50–4:10Close

[0:00 – 0:25] Hook / Problem

0:00 – 0:25
"Job hunting is exhausting. You spend hours tailoring your resume for every role, writing cover letters from scratch, and guessing what questions might come up in the interview — and most of the time, you don't even hear back.

What if you could go from a blank resume to a complete, customised application kit in under 30 seconds?

That's exactly what I built."

[0:25 – 0:50] Introduce the App

0:25 – 0:50
Show the login page on screen
"This is Job Application Co-Pilot — an AI-powered web app that takes your resume PDF and a job description, and runs four specialised AI agents in parallel to produce a complete application kit tailored to that specific role.

It's a full-stack application built with FastAPI on the backend, plain HTML and CSS on the frontend, and Groq's LLM API powering the agents. The backend is deployed on Render with a PostgreSQL database, and the frontend is hosted on Netlify — fully accessible from any browser."

[0:50 – 1:20] Sign In & Dashboard

0:50 – 1:20
Log in and show the dashboard
"After signing in, you land on the dashboard. You can see all your applications at a glance — the status, whether the AI kit has been generated, and quick summary stats across your pipeline."
Point to the stat cards — Total, Applied, Interviewing, Offers
"You can filter by status — not applied yet, applied, interviewing, rejected, or offer received — and update each application's status as you progress through the process."

[1:20 – 2:00] Create a New Application

1:20 – 2:00
Click "+ New Application"
"Creating a new application is a three-step wizard."
Step 1 — drop zone
"First, upload your resume as a PDF."
Drag and drop or browse — show the file confirmed
Step 2 — job details form
"Then paste in the job title, company, and the full job description. The more detail you give it, the better the output."
Fill in the form
Click "Generate My Kit" — show Step 3 progress screen
"The agents run in parallel — fit analysis first, then resume rewrite, cover letter, and interview prep simultaneously. The whole pipeline takes around 15 to 30 seconds."

[2:00 – 3:20] AI Kit Walkthrough (4 tabs)

2:00 – 2:20 — Fit Analysis
Navigate to the application — Fit Analysis tab opens
"The Fit Analysis agent scores how well your resume matches this role — in this case [X]%. It breaks down which requirements you already have — shown in green — and which gaps you'd need to address, in red."
Point to the score ring and requirement pills
"It also surfaces the key JD keywords and skills to emphasise — so you know exactly what language to mirror when talking to the recruiter."
2:20 – 2:40 — Resume Rewrite
Click Resume tab
"The Resume Rewrite agent takes your existing bullet points and rewrites them — injecting JD keywords, strengthening the impact language, and aligning phrasing with what the hiring team is looking for."
Show the diff view — red strikethrough on left, green rewritten on right
"You get a side-by-side diff so you can see exactly what changed and why. Download it as a text file and paste it back into your CV."
2:40 – 2:55 — Cover Letter
Click Cover Letter tab
"The Cover Letter agent writes a full one-page letter, personalised to the company and role. You can download it as a .docx — ready to attach to the application."
2:55 – 3:20 — Interview Prep
Click Interview Prep tab
"Finally, the Interview Prep agent generates 10 targeted questions — behavioural, technical, and situational — drawn from your resume and the job description."
Open one accordion card
"Each one comes with a sample answer and a tip on how to deliver it. Real preparation, not generic advice."

[3:20 – 3:50] Deployment

3:20 – 3:50
"The entire application is deployed to the cloud. The FastAPI backend runs on Render as a web service connected to a managed PostgreSQL database. The frontend — four static HTML pages — is served from Netlify.

Environment variables handle secrets: the Groq API key, JWT secret, and the database URL. The backend auto-detects PostgreSQL in production and SQLite locally — so the developer experience stays frictionless."

[3:50 – 4:10] Close

3:50 – 4:10
"Job Application Co-Pilot demonstrates a full-stack AI pipeline — multi-agent orchestration, JWT auth, file handling, PDF parsing, document generation, and cloud deployment — all in a single cohesive product.

The goal was simple: make applying for jobs feel less like a full-time job.

Thank you."

Recording Tips

SettingRecommendation
ToolLoom (loom.com) — free, records screen + webcam, instant shareable link. OBS works too.
Resolution1080p, 30fps minimum
Mouse speedKeep movements slow and deliberate — viewers need to follow where you click
Tab switchesPause 1–2 seconds before switching tabs so the transition reads clearly on screen
Demo dataUse a pre-seeded account with 2–3 existing applications so the dashboard looks populated
Resume & JDHave a real PDF resume and a real job description ready to paste — AI output with real data looks far more impressive
Score ringA match score of 65–80% looks most realistic and interesting to explain
Pro tip: Record yourself doing a dry run first (don't submit it). Watch it back at 1.5× speed and note where you hesitate or click too fast. The real recording will be significantly smoother.
